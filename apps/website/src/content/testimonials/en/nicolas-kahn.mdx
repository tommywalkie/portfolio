I was Tom's direct manager for a year at [La mètis](#la-m-tis), where he worked as a fullstack JavaScript developer on Rnest, our AI-powered strategic decision-support platform.

Tom quickly got up to speed with the web application's architecture and the frameworks we used. The context was demanding: he took over the work of our former external contractor without any break in continuity, and then became the only developer dedicated to the web side.

He went on to design and build features end to end, including a research assistant integrated into Rnest. He was also proactive beyond the code. For example, he introduced demo videos for new features, used both internally and for customer support.

I particularly appreciated the way he welcomed feedback. Within a few months, he became noticeably clearer in presenting his work and his progress.

Tom remained fully committed until his last day. During his final month, he single-handedly built an application that lets our analysts build relationship graphs from the data identified in our corpora. Beyond the technical work, he took users' needs into account and incorporated their feedback throughout development.

His departure was due to an economic restructuring of the company, not to the quality of his work. I recommend Tom for a fullstack developer role: he is a reliable, curious developer who pays close attention to users' needs.
